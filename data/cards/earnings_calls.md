# Data card: fictional earnings calls

- **Purpose:** extraction, classification, calibration, retrieval, and capstone labs.
- **Source:** generated templates and seeded numerical processes.
- **Status:** entirely fictional; ticker symbols and company names are invented.
- **Coverage:** four issuers, quarterly observations from 2021Q1 through 2026Q1.
- **Availability:** `available_at` is later than `published_at`; use availability for decisions.
- **Labels:** generated from revenue growth, margin change, guidance change, and noise.
- **Units:** margins are percentage points; `margin_change_bps` uses
  `100 basis points = 1 percentage point`.
- **Limitations:** templated language is easier than real transcripts and is not evidence of live-model performance.
- **License note:** generated specifically for this educational suite; retain the repository license.
